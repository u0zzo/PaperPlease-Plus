DEFAULT
	# Used for default responses. Unlike other traveler specifications, conditionals
	# here are based on the current Env and can access all facts. Also, conditionals
	# are evaluated at the moment when a response is needed, not when a traveler
	# is created as with other specifications.
	? Traveler/Nationality != Arstotzka
		? Day/Rules has RuleNeedEntryPermit || Day/Rules has RuleNeedAccessPermit
			? Traveler/Purpose == immigrate || Traveler/Purpose == diplomat
				> Intro
					" $ask-purpose
					" $state-purpose
			? Traveler/Purpose == asylum
				> Intro
					" $ask-purpose
					" $state-purpose
					@ Fingerprint
			> Intro
				" $ask-purpose
				" $state-purpose
				" $ask-duration
				" $state-duration

	? Traveler/PassportConfiscated != YES
		> Approved
			" $ask-approved
	? Traveler/PassportConfiscated == YES
		> Approved
			" Where is my passport? ; My passport? ; Passport? ; What is this slip? Where is passport?
			" @It is being confiscated.
			" How will I travel? ; What should I do? ; Why? ; You cannot do this.
			" @Contact number on slip.
			"
		> Denied
			" Where is my passport? ; My passport? ; Passport? ; What is this slip? Where is passport?
			" @It is being confiscated.
			" How will I travel? ; What should I do? ; Why? ; You cannot do this.
			" @Contact number on slip.
